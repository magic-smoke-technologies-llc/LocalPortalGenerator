# Local Portal Generator

An Abiotic Factor mod: a craftable item that opens a portal to any Portal World the players have already visited, so they don't have to travel there the long way.

## Language

**Generator**:
The craftable deployable a player builds and places at base. It is the only way to open a Portal, and it must be powered to do so.
_Avoid_: Portal generator item, device, machine, teleporter

**Portal**:
The opening a Generator creates beside itself, leading one way to one chosen Portal World's Entrance. It stays open until someone closes it at the Generator. Coming back uses that Portal World's own exit.
_Avoid_: Gate, rift, teleporter, warp

**Portal World**:
A separate place reached through a portal, such as Flathill, Night Realm or Mirror World. These are the only destinations the generator can reach.
_Avoid_: World, level, map, vignette

**Entrance**:
The fixed spot in a Portal World where a traveller arrives, the same spot its own portal lands you. A Portal World without an Entrance (Distant Shore, Tile) is not a destination.
_Avoid_: Spawn, start point, landing

**Visited**:
A Portal World is Visited once anyone in the save has been there, including visits made before the mod was installed wherever the game recorded them. Visited is shared by every player in that save, and only a Visited Portal World can be a destination.
_Avoid_: Discovered, unlocked, known

**Sector**:
A part of the Facility, such as Office, Labs, Dam or Security. Sectors are not destinations for the generator.
_Avoid_: World, area, zone
